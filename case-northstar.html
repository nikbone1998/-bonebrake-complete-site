02 / Home services · HVAC

Northstar Heating & Air

A fictional HVAC concept designed around a very different buyer than Aurelia: a homeowner may be cold, hot, stressed, comparing providers on a phone, and ready to call. The interface prioritizes speed, clarity, and practical trust.

StatusConcept project
IndustryHVAC / home services
Primary goalFast service decisions
ConversionCall / request service
The challenge

Home-service websites often bury the answer behind company history.

The concept assumes a visitor who needs to know three things quickly: whether the company handles the problem, whether it feels trustworthy, and how to get help. The architecture therefore reduces decorative friction and makes service categories, phone actions, financing reassurance, and proof visible early.

Urgency

Phone access stays obvious.

Calling is treated as a core task, especially on mobile, instead of a small header detail.

Services

Use decision language.

Heating, cooling, maintenance, and installation are separated so a homeowner can self-route without reading a long services page.

Trust

Proof near decisions.

Reviews, credentials, service expectations, and financing cues belong near the moments where doubt occurs.

Conversion

Two practical actions.

Call now for immediate needs or request service when the visitor prefers a form.

Mobile strategy

The small screen gets a different priority order.

On mobile, brand storytelling is compressed behind service relevance and contactability. Tap targets are large, service names remain readable, and the primary phone/request actions do not require hunting through a navigation drawer.

Design rationale

Professional without looking corporate.

The concept uses stronger utility, denser service information, and more direct action than the luxury work while still avoiding the cluttered “coupon flyer” look common in the category.

Visual system

Practical hierarchy.

High-contrast service labels, clear dividers, and structured modules support fast scanning.

Content

Answer homeowner questions.

Copy prioritizes services, expectations, financing, and next steps over generic claims.

Performance

Keep the service path light.

Phase 12 specifically targets the previous oversized implementation so the concept does not need megabytes of embedded markup to feel premium.

Objective

Reduce hesitation.

The concept is designed to help a visitor decide whether to call or request service with less uncertainty.
