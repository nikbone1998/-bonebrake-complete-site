One experienced team

A contractor for the whole property—not one narrow trade.

Dardon Construction is a second-generation roofing and general contractor serving Chicagoland. The company handles projects ranging from roofing, siding and gutters to concrete, brick work, remodeling, garages, additions and new construction.

That breadth matters when a property needs more than one repair. Residential, commercial and industrial clients can bring smaller work or larger construction needs to one experienced Chicago-based team.
