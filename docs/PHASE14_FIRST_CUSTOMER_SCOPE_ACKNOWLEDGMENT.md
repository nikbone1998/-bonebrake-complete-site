## Project

Bonebrake Web Design will provide one **Website Rebuild Pilot** for a one-time project price of **$1,995**, subject to the confirmed scope below. Full payment is required before production work begins.

The planning target for the first private preview is typically **3–7 days after complete intake and required assets are received**. This is a planning target, not a guaranteed deadline.

## Included scope

- responsive small-business website rebuild;
- information architecture and visual design;
- mobile-first implementation;
- client-supplied business copy/facts incorporated and refined as appropriate;
- contact calls-to-action using client-supplied contact information;
- semantic/technical SEO foundations and metadata;
- optional use of one client logo and up to six client-approved business photos;
- private QA-passed preview before launch;
- **two consolidated client revision rounds**;
- final client approval;
- launch assistance to the agreed domain after DNS/SSL readiness and Bonebrake owner approval;
- no required monthly maintenance retainer.

## Not included unless added in writing

Ecommerce, portals/logins, memberships, custom databases or applications, regulated sensitive-data forms, large SEO migrations, ongoing SEO campaigns, paid advertising, custom photography, logo/brand creation, unsupported integrations, large content migrations, third-party subscription fees, or ongoing maintenance.

Work materially outside the agreed rebuild is new scope and requires a separate quote/approval before work continues.

## Revision policy

The project includes **two consolidated revision rounds**. Each round is one organized batch of feedback on the current private preview. Only one revision round may be open at a time. Additional revision rounds or new-scope requests are quoted separately.

## Client materials and factual accuracy

The client confirms that business information supplied to Bonebrake is accurate to the best of the client's knowledge and that the client owns or has permission to use logos, photographs, copy, and other materials supplied for the website.

Bonebrake will not knowingly invent testimonials, reviews, awards, certifications, client counts, years in business, guarantees, addresses, prices, or performance claims that the client did not provide or approve.

## Domain and third parties

The client will provide reasonable cooperation/access needed for domain and DNS changes. Domain registration, third-party subscriptions, paid platforms, email/SMS providers, premium APIs, advertising, and other external service charges are not included unless explicitly stated in writing.

## Approval and launch

Client approval of the private preview does **not** itself publish the site. Bonebrake performs separate release, domain/SSL, integrity, and production checks before launch. Production remains under explicit Bonebrake owner control during the first-customer pilot.

## After launch

There is no required monthly maintenance plan. Immediate implementation defects identified around launch are triaged before new-scope enhancements. Future edits or additions are separate work unless explicitly included above.

## Cancellation / refunds

Any cancellation or refund terms for the specific project must be confirmed in writing before checkout if they are not already governed by a signed project agreement or the applicable checkout terms. Do not invent or promise a refund policy verbally.
