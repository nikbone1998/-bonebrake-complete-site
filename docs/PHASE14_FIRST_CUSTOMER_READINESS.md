# Phase 14 — First-Customer Readiness Standard

## Purpose

This is the operating standard for Bonebrake Web Design's first real paid pilot customer. It does not activate prospecting, outreach, checkout, fulfillment, or production. The pilot remains limited to one paid project and one concurrent project.

## Pilot offer

**Bonebrake Website Rebuild Pilot — $1,995 one-time.**

Full payment is required before production work begins. For a complete intake and supplied assets, the planning target for the first private preview is typically **3–7 days**. This is a target, not a guaranteed deadline or SLA.

Included:
- one polished responsive small-business website rebuild;
- information architecture and page/section planning;
- custom visual direction and mobile-first implementation;
- contact calls-to-action using client-supplied details;
- semantic HTML, metadata, accessibility, and technical SEO foundations;
- optional client logo and up to six approved business photographs;
- deterministic structural/safety QA;
- a private client preview;
- **two consolidated client revision rounds**;
- client approval plus separate owner release approval;
- launch assistance to the approved existing/custom domain after DNS/SSL readiness;
- no required monthly maintenance retainer.

Requested "pages" in the pilot build may be represented as clearly navigable full sections within the self-contained production document unless a separately agreed scope says otherwise.

## First-customer fit

Accept only a customer that meets all of these conditions:
- established local or service business;
- informational or lead-generation website is the primary need;
- an actual decision-maker can approve scope and launch;
- business can provide accurate facts, contact details, service information, and domain/DNS access;
- business can provide or affirm rights to any logo/photos it asks Bonebrake to use;
- business can review the private preview and return consolidated feedback promptly;
- project does not require regulated sensitive-data collection.

Do not use the first pilot for:
- ecommerce or complex checkout;
- customer portals, memberships, logins, or custom applications/databases;
- regulated medical, financial, legal, or other sensitive-data forms;
- large SEO migrations or an ongoing SEO campaign;
- paid advertising management;
- custom photography or logo creation;
- unsupported third-party integrations;
- a giant content migration;
- same-day emergency launch requirements.

## Scope boundary

The $1,995 pilot is a website rebuild, not an unlimited digital transformation engagement. New services, materially new page sets, ecommerce, application logic, new integrations, branding creation, or work that was not reasonably part of the agreed rebuild is **new scope** and must stop for a separate written quote/approval.

### Revision policy

Two consolidated client revision rounds are included.

A revision round is one organized batch of feedback against the current private preview. Only one client revision round may be open at a time. The system will not accept a third included client revision round. Rejected, cancelled, or technical-failure revision attempts do not consume the client's included allowance.

The customer should review the whole preview before submitting a round. Piecemeal messages are consolidated before work begins.

## Client responsibilities

Before production work can move normally, the client is responsible for:
- providing truthful and current business information;
- identifying the desired website goal, priority services/products, ideal customer, and visual direction;
- supplying only logos/photos/content they own or are authorized to use;
- identifying required pages/sections and contact details;
- providing domain/DNS cooperation when launch begins;
- reviewing the private preview and giving consolidated feedback;
- approving the final QA-passed preview before release.

Bonebrake does not fabricate testimonials, reviews, awards, certifications, client counts, years in business, guarantees, addresses, prices, or performance claims that were not supplied and approved.

## Customer lifecycle

1. **Manual acquisition / qualification** — owner identifies or receives one candidate. No automated prospecting/outreach in pilot mode.
2. **Fit confirmation** — confirm the project fits this standard and send the scope acknowledgment before checkout.
3. **Checkout** — send only the dedicated one-customer $1,995 checkout after all live readiness gates are green. The Stripe link is single-use.
4. **Payment verification** — webhook verifies the paid state and binds only the allowed pilot customer/project.
5. **Private intake** — client answers the five required questions and may upload one logo plus up to six JPEG/PNG/WebP business photos, max 5 MB each.
6. **Build preparation** — approved intake and asset references become the generation specification. Arbitrary external asset URLs are prohibited.
7. **Generation + QA** — AI worker creates only a private preview. Structural/safety QA must pass before client approval is enabled.
8. **Client review** — client approves or uses one of two consolidated revision rounds.
9. **Revision** — each round requires owner approval, creates a new preview version, and reruns QA.
10. **Client approval** — client approval creates a release candidate but does not publish.
11. **Owner release approval** — separate owner decision marks the candidate release-ready.
12. **Domain onboarding** — authoritative DNS and SSL must verify.
13. **Production approval** — separate production action and production switch are required. Pilot mode never implies automatic production.
14. **Launch verification** — resolver hash/smoke checks must match the approved artifact. Rollback path remains available.
15. **Completion** — confirm launch state and move any future changes to separately quoted work.

## Support standard during pilot

The pilot is manually supervised by the owner. Use a **one-business-day internal response target** for ordinary client questions; this is an operating target, not a 24/7 SLA or contractual guarantee. Safety, payment ambiguity, production failure, or domain incidents take priority.

Do not promise continuous support, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed conversion lifts, or guaranteed delivery dates.

## Post-launch

There is **no required maintenance retainer**. Immediate implementation defects discovered around launch are triaged before new-scope requests. Future edits, new pages/services, integrations, SEO campaigns, redesigns, or enhancements are quoted separately.

Third-party costs such as domains, paid platforms, email/SMS providers, premium APIs, advertising, or other subscriptions are not included unless explicitly written into scope.

## Automatic halt conditions

Keep or restore the global external-effects lock when any of these occurs:
- payment state is ambiguous;
- wrong customer/project is claimed;
- critical/error incident opens;
- dead-letter/manual intervention affects the pilot;
- AI output fails safety/quality checks;
- client assets cannot be validated;
- production artifact integrity fails;
- domain/SSL readiness becomes uncertain;
- unexpected external effects occur;
- owner chooses to halt.

## Owner rule

A successful first project is evidence, not permission to remove caps. Customer #2 or autonomous acquisition requires a separately reviewed expansion decision.
