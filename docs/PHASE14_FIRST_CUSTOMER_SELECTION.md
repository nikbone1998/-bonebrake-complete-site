# Phase 14 — First-Customer Selection

Status: **PREPARED / NOT CONTACTED**

This document selects the preferred first real Bonebrake Website Rebuild Pilot candidate. It does not activate prospecting, outreach, checkout, payments, fulfillment, or production. No Apollo credits were used.

## Selection criteria

A first-customer candidate should maximize the odds of a clean, valuable pilot rather than merely having the weakest website.

Weighted criteria:
- established operating business and credible market history — 20
- service/lead-generation website fits the $1,995 pilot — 20
- visible website opportunity without requiring a giant migration — 20
- enough reputation/business activity to plausibly value the rebuild — 15
- public direct contact path without Apollo — 10
- clear business facts/assets available for a preview — 10
- low complexity / low compliance risk — 5

## Ranked shortlist

### 1. Dardon Construction, Inc. — 92/100 — PREFERRED CUSTOMER #1

Public website: https://www.dardonconstruction.com/
Public phone: 866-327-3661
Public email: info@dardonconstruction.com
Public address: 3925 W Armitage Ave, Chicago, IL 60647

Why it fits:
- family-owned Chicago contractor with a long public operating history and “since 1969” positioning;
- licensed roofing/general-contractor business with strong trust material already available;
- BBB Accredited / A+ public profile;
- sells higher-value roofing, exterior, construction and remodeling work where one incremental qualified lead can materially exceed the website price;
- current site is functional but significantly understates the company’s credibility and breadth;
- service page is largely a grid of project/service images rather than a conversion-oriented service architecture;
- homepage has useful trust material, testimonials, financing, licensing and contact details but lacks a strong hierarchy that turns those facts into a premium sales narrative;
- project photography/gallery already exists, lowering asset friction for a pilot;
- no ecommerce, portal, membership, sensitive-data or custom-application requirement is apparent;
- direct business phone and email are public, so no Apollo enrichment is necessary.

Primary redesign thesis:
> Dardon already looks credible as a contractor in the real world. The website should make that credibility immediately obvious online.

Recommended preview direction:
- roofing/exterior credibility first, broader construction capability second;
- premium hero emphasizing family ownership, Chicagoland history and licensed/bonded/insured positioning;
- immediate Call / Request an Estimate CTAs;
- trust rail for licenses, longevity, financing and service area;
- structured service architecture for Roofing, Siding & Gutters, Masonry & Concrete, Remodeling, Garages/Additions/New Construction, and Restoration;
- project gallery with service/category context rather than an undifferentiated image wall;
- testimonial proof section;
- simple “how the project works” sequence;
- strong mobile contact path;
- no unsupported claims or invented metrics.

Risks / cautions:
- broad service mix means the first preview must stay focused rather than becoming a giant content migration;
- do not repeat self-reported or third-party claims as fact unless they are present on the company’s own site or confirmed by the client;
- existing testimonials/photos should be treated as client-owned/source material and formally confirmed during intake before production use.

Decision: **Prepare Dardon-specific preview and manual owner-reviewed outreach. Do not send until the preview is ready and owner explicitly approves contact.**
