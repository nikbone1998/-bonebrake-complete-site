04 / Automotive service

Westside Auto Lab

A fictional automotive-service concept built around competence and certainty. The direction avoids both sterile dealership polish and noisy performance-shop clichés, using a tougher but disciplined visual system to support service understanding and booking.

StatusConcept project
IndustryAutomotive service
Primary goalExpertise + clarity
ConversionBook / call
The challenge

Technical expertise is useless online if the customer cannot tell what you actually service.

The concept organizes the business around recognizable customer needs—diagnostics, maintenance, brakes, suspension, performance and repair—then places credibility around those decisions. The visitor should not need to decode a brand manifesto before learning whether the shop can help.

Services

Specific beats broad.

Clear service categories make expertise easier to understand and improve the path from problem to booking.

Trust

Competence without bravado.

The visual language feels capable and technical without relying on exaggerated claims.

Navigation

Short and task-based.

Services, expertise, process, and booking remain the core choices.

Conversion

Booking remains visible.

The customer always has a straightforward path to schedule or call.

Visual direction

Mechanical confidence without visual noise.

High contrast, strong spacing, direct type, and workshop imagery give the project more edge than the other BWD concepts. Restraint matters: the design should make the business look controlled, not chaotic.

Decision system

Reduce uncertainty before the keys change hands.

Hierarchy

Problem before personality.

The visitor's service need gets priority over decorative brand storytelling.

Mobile

Booking and calling stay reachable.

Small-screen layout prioritizes service scanning and action rather than compressed desktop modules.

Proof

Show the environment.

Workshop and technician imagery can communicate seriousness when used deliberately and honestly.

Objective

Make the shop easier to choose.

The concept is designed to convert technical credibility into a clear customer decision.
